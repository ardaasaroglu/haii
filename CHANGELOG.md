# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public release.

* Reads a meeting from a transcript you paste, a Google Doc of meeting notes, or a Fireflies, Fathom or Otter transcript, and turns each thing you promised into a card that quotes the sentence you said.
* Ten action types: calendar event, email draft, to-do, Slack message, Jira issue, document, file share, CRM note, tracker task and text message. Each one runs only after your gesture, with a short undo.
* Writes a brief before a meeting from your calendar and the tools you connect.
* Runs on your own machine with an embedded database and your own Claude API key.
* A demo on a fictional week runs in the browser, at `?demo` locally and at <https://ardaasaroglu.github.io/haii/>.
