# Code of Conduct

Be kind to the people you meet here, in issues, pull requests and reviews. Argue about the code, never about the person, and assume good faith. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not welcome, and a maintainer may remove them or block whoever posts them.

To report a problem, use **Report content** in the menu of the comment, issue or pull request. Reports are private. Please do not argue it out in the thread. For a security problem, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
